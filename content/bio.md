Welcome to my personal website！I am an MPhil student in Artificial Intelligence at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. My research interests lie at the intersection of human-robot interaction and embodied intelligence

My research investigates world-state reasoning in human-robot interaction, especially how robots can detect mismatches between the assumptions underlying human instructions and the actual state of the environment before taking action. This work has led to a paper accepted by ACM Transactions on Human-Robot Interaction (THRI) in September 2026, where I study world-state mismatch detection, norm-aware intervention, and the integration of IoT sensing and LLM-based reasoning.

Now, I am also interested in exploring the relationship between emotion and robot action in both directions: how robot motions can express emotion, and how emotional states can influence the way an action is generated and performed. In daily life, even the same action can look very different depending on a person’s emotional state or personality. For example, knocking on a door can be gentle, hurried, or forceful, and a shy student may raise a hand very differently from an outgoing student who is eager to answer a question. These differences suggest that emotion is not only communicated through facial expressions or language, but is also embedded in motion itself through factors such as speed, force, rhythm, amplitude, timing, and posture. I am committed to making human-robot interaction emotional and warm, rather than cold metal. Contribute to the entry of robots into families and society!
